### A. Raw Fast %K Formulation

For a lookback window of $N_K$ bars (`InpKPeriod`):
$$HH_t = \max_{0 \le j < N_K}(H_{t-j}), \quad LL_t = \min_{0 \le j < N_K}(L_{t-j})$$
$$\text{Raw } \%K_t = \begin{cases} \left( \frac{C_t - LL_t}{HH_t - LL_t} \right) \cdot 100.0 & \text{if } (HH_t - LL_t) > 1.0 \times 10^{-9} \\ 50.0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

### B. Adaptive Chaos-Weighted Slowing Equation

The base smoothing alpha is derived from the slowing period:
$$\alpha_{\text{base}} = \frac{2}{\text{InpSlowingPeriod} + 1}$$

The non-linear modulation equation adjusts the responsiveness based on market entropy:
$$\alpha_{\text{adj}} = \max\Big( 0.005, \, \min\big( 1.0, \, \alpha_{\text{base}} \cdot (1.0 - Chaos_t)^{\text{InpSensitivity}} \big) \Big)$$

$$\%K_t = \%K_{t-1} + \alpha_{\text{adj}} \cdot (\text{Raw } \%K_t - \%K_{t-1})$$

* **In a Strong Trend ($Chaos_t \to 0.0$):** $(1.0 - 0.0)^{\text{Sens}} = 1.0 \implies \alpha_{\text{adj}} = \alpha_{\text{base}}$ (instantaneous tracking).
* **In Extreme Chaos ($Chaos_t \to 1.0$):** $(1.0 - 1.0)^{\text{Sens}} = 0.0 \implies \alpha_{\text{adj}} = 0.005$. The filter locks in place, acting as an automatic brake against choppy whipsaws.

### C. Signal Line (%D) Smoothing via `MovingAverage_Engine.mqh`

## 3. Range-Based Candle Source Architecture (`ENUM_CANDLE_SOURCE`)

Unlike single-price oscillators (such as RSI), a Stochastic oscillator is fundamentally a **range-bound, three-coordinate geometric indicator** requiring the full candle triplet ($\text{High}, \text{Low}, \text{Close}$).

`ChaosWeightedStochastic_Pro` aligns strictly with the suite's established candle-source standards:


* **`CANDLE_STANDARD` Mode:** Evaluates authentic physical exchange highs, lows, and closes. Chaos FDI is computed on the standard close series.
* **`CANDLE_HEIKIN_ASHI` Mode:** Evaluates direct in-place Heikin Ashi extremes ($HA\_High, HA\_Low, HA\_Close$). Micro-wicks are filtered out, generating pristine structural swing ranges and noise-free %K trajectories.

### The $p + d$ Non-Repainting Protocol

* A pivot at bar $p$ requires $d$ confirmation bars to the left and $d$ bars to the right (`InpDivStrength`).

* Therefore, a pivot is **permanently confirmed and locked** at bar $p + d$.
* Once confirmed, a trendline is drawn connecting the confirmed pivot points in the subwindow (`OBJ_TREND`, Style: `STYLE_DOT`).
* **Zero Repaint Guarantee:** The trendline coordinates are anchored to historical timestamps. They will never move or disappear on future ticks.

### A. True MTF Divergences (Eliminating the Stepped-Curve Paradox)

When viewing higher timeframe Stochastic on an execution chart (e.g., viewing H1 Stochastic on an M15 chart):

* Conventional indicators run divergence checks on the stepped lower timeframe curve, resulting in false triggers on flat steps.
* `ChaosWeightedStochastic_Pro` evaluates divergences **strictly in the clean, unstepped HTF domain** (`h_time[]`, `h_high[]`, `h_low[]`, `h_res_k[]`).
* Confirmed HTF divergence trendlines are anchored directly to the higher timeframe candle open timestamps (`h_time`), projecting clean, mathematically pure macro divergence lines onto the lower timeframe chart.


* Different instances will **never delete, overwrite, or collide with each other's graphical objects**.

* `GetIndicatorSubwindow()` automatically binds each instance's trendlines to its own designated subwindow.
